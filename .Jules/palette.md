## 2026-07-06 - Adding aria-label to Close buttons\n**Learning:** Found several modal/drawer close buttons across the app lacking an explicit `aria-label`, rendering them inaccessible to screen readers. Applying `aria-label="Close"` is a quick and effective accessibility fix for icon-only buttons.\n**Action:** Remember to consistently add `aria-label`s when creating new modal or side panel components with icon-only close buttons.
