    Architectural note. Your app is unusually well-positioned for a "personal life OS" pivot because the hard parts are already done: Google-OAuth-gated multi-user data scoping (X-User-Email partition), Web Push without SaaS (VAPID + service worker), per-minute cron, Mongo + per-user collections, SSE streaming, and an SWR-cached React frontend with PWA-capable shell. Most of the features below are module-shaped — a new backend/<module>/ folder with an api.py, a Mongo collection, a cron line, and a frontend route. The expensive integrations (HealthKit ingestion, Plaid/SimpleFin, voice capture) are best treated as ingest endpoints the client posts to, rather than SDKs to embed.
