# 📉 Down 40%+ tab (`?tab=fallen`) — 2026-10-02

## The ask, verbatim (Ajay, 2026-10-02)

1. "Can you build me a tab in chart maps about stocks that dropped more than 40% lowers from like app loving company as an example whcih si 60% low.
   But I also need you to capture informations about sales like Bondes and other indicators based on Bondes formula please."
2. "Scan the universe and bring me these stocks"
3. (mid-build) "also add things like possible catalyst that made is drop like that."

Reference point agreed before the build: the **52-week high** (the highest intraday high of the last 252 closed sessions). APP closed 271.73 on 2026-10-02 against 738.01 (2025-12-22): **63.18% below**.

**UNMEASURED.** No study in this app says a stock 40% or more under its 52-week high, with any count of Bonde's legs, does anything next. The order is a count and a distance, not a ranking. Nothing on this tab gates a scan, pushes a phone, sizes a position or enters a paper lane. Not advice.

## 💥 What hit it — possible, never proof

Per name: the `TOP_DROPS` = 3 most negative close-to-close sessions strictly **after** the 52-week high's day, through the last closed bar (ties: the later date first). Per drop: c2c %, gap % (open / prev close − 1), intraday % (close / open − 1), the larger leg, volume ÷ the mean of the 50 sessions before it (`momentum_burst.avg_volume_before`, none under 50), share of the fall = (prev − close) ÷ (52w high − last close), and the window `WINDOW_BEFORE` = 1 session before to `WINDOW_AFTER` = 1 after.

Each drop lists every item **on file** dated in its window — the stored text only, labelled "possible"; "nothing on file" when a source holds nothing. The 💥 line shows the most specific item in `PRIORITY` order: **earnings > medical > 8-K / offering > index deletion > news > analyst action > model read**, then the group numbers (sector ETF %, the theme's median member % with the name left out, RSP %). No "sector-wide day" verdict is printed while `GROUP_DAY_CUT is None`.

**Public by the drop's close** (2026-10-03, `fallen_catalysts.public_by_close`): an item dated before the drop day, or ON it and not an after-the-close (AMC) earnings report — and (2026-10-03, round 2) not a headline whose own published time is at or after `SESSION_CLOSE` (16:00 ET, imported from `supply_demand.demand_alerts`) that day: LQDA's "trending after hours" headline at 19:29 ET had been the 💥 pick over the 08:05 ET ruling. Only those can be the 💥 "possible" item; anything later in the window stays in the fold with the label "after the drop: …" (`AFTER_DROP`), and a drop with only later items reads "possible: nothing on file by that close". Inside one kind and day an analyst rating change (`up` / `down` / `init`, `ANALYST_CHANGE_ACTIONS`) leads a maintained / reiterated one, then the earliest published time (a headline keeps its epoch as `ts`; untimed items follow the timed ones), then the source's own order — never the alphabet. A price target stored as 0 is a missing target and is not printed. Neither the fold's head nor a drop day's own line prints a share of the fall above 100% (the name rallied between the drops — the line says "more than the high-to-close fall (it rallied in between)" instead; ARCT read 103.80% before 2026-10-03). The sources line prints each source's earliest item **on a listed name**, not the source's own horizon (the table below has the horizons).

## Payload

The served contract is spec §3.7 (`fallen_board` + per-tile `pick` and `fallen`): `state`, `session`, `as_of`, `built_at`, `stale_scan`, `measured`, `threshold_pct`, `year_bars`, `glitch_ratio`, `depth_pct`, `depth_param`, `depths`, `sort`, `header`, `order_line`, `count_line`, `note`, `counts`, `suspects`, `pick_legend`, `pick_coverage`, `catalysts`. Warming / error envelopes carry every key with `counts / suspects / pick_legend / pick_coverage / catalysts / built_at = null`. URL: `?tab=fallen[&sort=default|fallen_depth|fallen_sales|market_cap|market_cap_asc][&depth=40|50|60|70]`; any other sort or depth serves the default.

## Cost

- **Background build** (`fallen_tab.build`, one daemon thread per key, key = (session, universe, `scanner.latest_key()`)): ONE `bulk_cached_frames` read over the universe, ONE `scanner.load_latest_shared()` (the single-flight shared parse), ONE `bonde_picks.attach`, ONE cap read, ONE profile read, ONE read per 💥 source + ONE cached-frames read for the group ETFs / theme members not in the universe. Frames are dropped after the build; the memo holds the ~600 listed rows. Scout timing: frames + legs 4.1 s, catalysts 4 s. Stale-while-revalidate (`MEMO_TTL_SEC` = 1 h is cache freshness, not a rule); a failed build is served as an error and retried at most every `FAIL_RETRY_SEC`.
- **Per request** (`board.fallen_tiles`): one dict lookup, one filter + sort of the held rows, the liquidity floor, ONE `_bulk_snaps` over at most `limit + BAR_BUFFER` names, ONE `zone_store.load_latest`, then the standard `_finish` bar load. No scan parse, no universe snapshot, no Mongo read over the universe, no per-symbol fetch.
- Probe (read-only, throwaway container): `backend/scripts/fallen_tab_probe.py` — times the cold build and every served sort / depth, writes the payloads and a summary.

## HIS CALLS (defaults shipped; each is one constant)

1. **Order** = most Bonde legs read as PASS first, the deeper drop breaks a tie (`DEFAULT_SORT` → `fallen_tab.bonde_key`), and the card prints "N of M legs read pass". This bends the 2026-09-20 rule that no count over the legs is shown or sorted on (`bonde_picks.py:26-29`); the alternative is one flip: deepest first.
2. Reference = the 52-week high, intraday highs (`REFERENCE = "52w"`); the all-time high is the 🏔️ ATH engine if preferred.
3. Threshold = 40% (`THRESHOLD_PCT`), listed at ≥.
4. Depth view 40 / 50 / 60 / 70 (`DEPTH_STEPS`).
5. ETFs out (`INCLUDE_ETFS = False`).
6. Fewer than 252 sessions: counted, not listed (`LIST_SHORT_HISTORY = False`).
7. Data-error guard = the price layer's 5× one-session rule (`GLITCH_RATIO`). Real collapses (SION-type) can trip it — named, not hidden; 2:1–4:1 splits the cache never adjusted and 0.2×-type days pass it and can be listed.
8. No price / cap floor of the tab's own; the board's liquidity tier (default Tradeable, `board.DEFAULT_MIN_TIER`) applies, so most no-scan names sit under it.
9. 💥 for "What hit it" (`fallen_catalysts.HIT_MARK`) — 🧨 already marks the explosive read on every card.
10. Top 3 drop days (`TOP_DROPS`), window 1 before / 1 after (`WINDOW_BEFORE`, `WINDOW_AFTER`).
11. 💥 priority (`PRIORITY`); no "sector-wide day" verdict until a cut is given (`GROUP_DAY_CUT = None`).
12. No catalyst-class chip or filter (93.0% of drop days have nothing dated — probe 2026-10-03: 1,646 of 1,770).
13. Left out: Form 4 / 144 / 13D/G, the catalyst one-liner cache (its `is_llm` flag means "LLM enabled", not "a model wrote this"), `macro_context`, the frozen `bonde_fin` study file.
14. Warm jobs to close the gaps — **none shipped**; each needs his nod and a crontab line (a main deploy does NOT ship a crontab change): (a) SEC EDGAR 8-K items + S-1/S-3/424B for 52 weeks on the listed names via the paced `sepa.insider._edgar_get` (free, ~620 GETs); (b) `earnings_watch` keeping every past row it already fetches; (c) dated news windows (Finnhub company-news from/to on the shared key, or Massive news — paid); (d) `analyst_pulse.refresh` for the uncovered names; (e) Massive splits (`corporate_actions.fetch_splits`, paid) to drop unadjusted-split days. Index deletions before 2026-08-23 exist nowhere.
15. No-scan names: Bonde's scan legs read "not read"; a research-cache fallback is not shipped.
16. Push: none. No paper lane, no gate, no sizing.
17. Tab slot right after 🛡️ Resiliency.
18. 🎯 filter is n/a on this tab (chips show, nothing hides) — `fallen` is not in `enterable.KIND_BY_TAB`.
19. One chart marker, on the largest drop day.
20. The 📈 Bonde tab's measured banner is not shown here (it measures Episodic Pivots, not this list).
21. Card cap = shares cache × last closed close, not board_metrics (the `cap_10b` leg keeps bonde_picks' own source).
22. Closed bars roll at 20:00 ET (`KL.ROLL_AT`), not 16:30: from 16:00 to 20:00 the tab still reads the prior close.
23. Card cap → **the cap badge is left off** (`market_cap` null; the card then reads like a name the shares cache never saw) when shares × close and the shares doc's own `market_cap` differ by `CAP_DISAGREE_RATIO` (the imported 5× `GLITCH_RATIO`) or more (2026-10-03; BYND, UWMC). Alternatives: prefer the doc's own `market_cap` in that case, or a different ratio. Display only — it changes no DM 💰 cap and gates nothing.
