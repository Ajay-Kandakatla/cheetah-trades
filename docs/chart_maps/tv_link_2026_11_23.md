`chartOnly` tiles (the charts inside a Watch tile) draw no action row, so no link-out there — the tile head carries it.

## The URL

`https://www.tradingview.com/chart/?symbol=SYM[&interval=X]`

- Helper `frontend/src/lib/tvChart.ts` — recreated from `git show 70a625b9^:frontend/src/lib/tvChart.ts` (Ajay
  2026-08-31 *"also give me a trading view pre configured chart please"*): trim, upper-case, the dash share class → the dot
  TradingView writes (`BRK-B` → `BRK.B`), `encodeURIComponent`.
- Frame → interval, the 2026-08-31 map unchanged: `1min`→1, `5m_today`/`24h`→5, `15m`→15, `60m`→60, `daily`→D,
  `weekly`→W (retired `5m_live`→5, `15m_open`→15). **One change from 08-31:** an unknown or absent frame now sends **no**
  `interval` (it used to fall back to daily).
- Only the symbol and the interval ride. No user data, no email, no account id.
- Buttons call `openTvChart(e, sym, tf?)`: `preventDefault` + `stopPropagation` (a tile is a router `<Link>` / the ⤢
  opener), then `window.open(url, '_blank', 'noopener,noreferrer')`. The Support head is an `<a target="_blank"
  rel="noopener noreferrer">`.

## What it can NOT do

- **None of our lines travel.** No support / demand zones, no entry / stop lines, no 🔑 levels, no Gajjala marks — TradingView
  draws only what HIS TradingView layout holds. The two charts can disagree on zones; ours are the ones the alerts use.
- **The 5-day range, EMAs and VWAP are his TradingView setup, not ours.** The URL cannot set indicators or a date range;
  he saves them once in his TradingView layout (e.g. EMA 9, EMA 21, VWAP; range "5D") and every `TV ↗` opens on it.
- **Setup note (third-party pricing comparisons, not a TradingView statement we verified):** the free TradingView Basic
  plan is listed at **2 indicators per chart** (Essential 5, Plus 10, Premium 25) by
  [CMC Markets](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en-gb/trading-platforms/tradingview/tradingview-pricing-explained) and
  [Equiti](https://www.equiti.com/uae-en/news/trading-ideas/is-tradingview-free-plans-pricing-and-free-vs-paid-compared/).
  So EMA 9 + EMA 21 + VWAP (three) needs a paid plan, or one combined community script that draws all three. (An older
  review says 3 on free; treat that as stale.)
- The interval on a 👀 Watch tile is not set on purpose (three frames, one button).
