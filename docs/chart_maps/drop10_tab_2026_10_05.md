# 🔻 Down 10%+ today — Chart Maps tab (2026-10-05)

**Ask (Ajay 2026-10-03, verbatim):** "I would like to know about stocks that falled intraday more than 10% new tab please."

URL: `https://chartmaptrading.com/chart-maps?tab=drop10[&sort=default|drop10_now|drop10_reclaim|drop10_rvol]`

**Status: UNMEASURED.** Display only. Nothing here gates a scan, pushes a phone, sizes a position or enters a lane.

## The list

The tab lists every universe stock whose session **low** is `THRESHOLD_PCT` (10) % or more below the **prior session's close**. Both of these are listed and labelled:

- **still down**: the print is still 10%+ below the prior close.
- **reclaimed**: the low reached −10%, and the print has since come back above that line.

The prior close is the cached daily frame's last closed bar before the session (`zone_store.drop_today`). If a name's cached bars end before the prior market day, it is counted as `stale`. Its prior close is never guessed.

## Which session (the tape clock, `zone_edge.session_state`)

| clock | mode | source |
|---|---|---|
| 09:30–16:00 ET, market day | `live` | today's Massive snapshot day bar: one fanned-out bulk call over the universe, built in a background thread |
| 16:00–20:00 ET | `after_close` | the same snapshot day bar, which is the regular session. Its `close` is the 16:00 close: on the 2026-10-05 probe, IART read 12.68, the cached close, while the last after-hours trade was 12.89 |
| pre-market, overnight, weekends, holidays | `closed` | the last closed session, read off the cached daily frames |

A half day after its 13:00 close counts as `after_close` (`momentum_burst.burst_session`).

**Trap: the snapshot's `date` field.** Overnight, `bulk_snapshot` still carries the last session's bar but stamps it with today's date, because there is no `day.t`. A snapshot row is therefore read only when its own minute stamp (`min_t_ms`) is dated the session. If that stamp is missing, the last-trade stamp (`last_trade_ts_ms`, in ns) is used instead.

## Held out, counted, named under the grid

- ETFs and delisted fates. They are counted only when they *would* have been listed.
- A low or print that the price layer's own 5× guard flags (`prices._is_scale_glitch`).
- A live prior close that differs from the cached one by more than `PREV_CLOSE_TOL_PCT` (1%). This catches a split or a bad print.
- A split that Massive lists as executed on the session (`drop10_tab.fetch_splits_strict`, `/v3/reference/splits`). It is read once per listed name per session. Unlike `portfolio.corporate_actions.fetch_splits`, it raises on a non-200, so an outage is counted `split_unchecked` and retried, never read as "no split".

## Each card

- **The pill:** `🔻 −21.50% at the low · still down`, or `· reclaimed to −6.93%`.
- **Stats:**
  - **Prior close** (and its date)
  - **Low**
  - **Now**
  - **Legs:** the gap against open → low
  - **Volume** against the 50 closed sessions before it (`momentum_burst.rvol_leg`). In RTH the full-session projection is used whenever one exists, once the projection start has passed. Before that, it reads "so far". `rvol_leg` is a gate, and once volume clears its bar it drops the projection; the tab keeps the projection so the 📊 sort never inverts (critic 2026-10-05).
  - **Group:** what the sector ETF, the theme's median member and RSP did that session (`fallen_catalysts.group_day`).
- **The demand band and room**, from the 🏎️ zone read (`dual_momentum_tab.zone_read`), the same as on 📉.
- **💥 What hit it** (`fallen_catalysts.items_for` / `hit_text`): the head is the fall to the low, and its larger leg is the gap or open → low, whichever did the falling (on a reclaimed name too). It then shows what is on file from the session before through this session, most specific kind first. It is labelled *possible*. Anything out after 16:00 ET on the session is labelled "after the drop" and is never the pick.
- **🩳 short interest**, rendered by the client chip on every board.

## Known gaps

- A snapshot chunk that the fan-out swallowed is asked once more. Names still unanswered are counted `no_snapshot` and printed in the count line, never folded into "no print".
- **Closed mode trusts the cached session bar.** If the host slept through the 16:30 patch, an hourly in-progress bar could be read as the closed session (critic 2026-10-05, owner's call). This is the same exposure the 📉 tab has.

## Orders (served, tab-scoped)

| key | label |
|---|---|
| `default` | 🔻 Deepest at the low first |
| `drop10_now` | 📉 Deepest now first |
| `drop10_reclaim` | 🔄 Most reclaimed off the low first |
| `drop10_rvol` | 📊 Volume vs normal first (no read sits last) |

The liquidity floor is the board's usual one. The default is Tradeable, ≥ $10M/day on the 50 sessions before the session.

## Memo

- One background build, keyed by (mode, session, universe) and served stale-while-revalidate.
- Freshness: `LIVE_TTL_SEC` = 60 s in RTH, `CLOSED_TTL_SEC` = 30 min otherwise. Both are cache freshness, not rules.
- The per-session base (prior close, 50-day averages) is memoised for `BASE_TTL_SEC` (1 h).
- The page re-reads a **live** board on the served `refresh_sec`, floored at 15 s, while the tab is visible.
- Measured on 2026-10-05 in a throwaway container: 2,743 names; the closed build took 8.5 s and the live rebuild 1.4 s on the warm base.

## Verified on real data (2026-10-02 session)

8 of 2,743 stocks were listed: **IART** (−21.5% at the low, gap −17.3%, headline on file), **STX**, **WDC** (XLK +1.01%, the ai_semis median +2.17%, so a name-specific drop), **THRM**, **WSHP**, **ACH**, **EOLS**, **GEMI**. 3 were still down and 5 reclaimed.

Under the default floor, 5 show: IART, STX, WDC, THRM, GEMI. Both the closed path and the live path (run against the snapshot that still held Friday) gave the same 8. They also match the 2026-10-03 probe.
