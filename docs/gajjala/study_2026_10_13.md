## No look-ahead — how it is enforced and pinned
- A detector at pass L sees only bars whose right label is ≤ L; the 9/21 EMAs and K1's average are causal, so the cut
  frame equals the frame a live pass would build at L (`test_prefix_frame_is_the_frame_a_pass_would_build`).
- Truncating the 1-minute input after any minute m leaves every event whose entry bar closed by m unchanged — setup,
  entry bar, class, trigger, stop, fill minute, window AND the pass that saw it
  (`test_no_lookahead_truncating_after_any_minute_keeps_earlier_events`, seven cut points).
- Rewriting everything after the entry (a collapse) moves only the OUTCOME, never the event.
- Every event is seen at a pass at or after its entry bar's close, and filled inside that bar.
- Mutation check (2026-10-13): handing the detectors one bar more than the pass may see, or the whole day, turns those
  tests red (1 and 8 failures respectively).
- The placebo never samples a bar before q (`test_placebo_pool_only_from_a_no_setup_name_and_never_before_q`).

## What it does NOT know (in every report)
- **Float.** No point-in-time float exists here; 🎾 is measured on its STRUCTURE with the ≤ 10M float gate NOT applied
  (`STUDY_FLOAT`). Today's yfinance float on a 2025 tape would be look-ahead and wrong after reverse splits.
- **Instrument types**: a ticker the ACTIVE map knows is typed by today's entity (a reused ticker reads its current
  type). A name only the dated lookup could not type stays "type unknown" and is kept (round A); the count is in meta and
  the whole table is printed a second time with those names dropped (`report_known_types`).
- **Half days**: `timeframes.HALF_DAYS` starts 2026-11-27; the study skips the three it knows are missing and flags a
  window that starts before `HALF_DAYS_CHECKED_FROM` (2025-01-01) — earlier half days were not checked.
- **The 16:00 minute** carries the closing cross and is tagged after-hours (trap T7): the `session` exit's "16:00 close"
  is the 15:59 minute's close.
- **Grouped-daily coverage**: the superset trusts the grouped bar's high / volume; if Massive's grouped bar leaves out
  extended hours, a pre-market-only qualifier is missed. The 1-minute q check is the real gate either way.
- **Splits**: every fetch is `adjusted=false` (as printed) — see the 2026-10-13 paragraph below; the live scanner's
  split hold-out is not replayed.
- **Slippage, halts, borrow**: none modelled. Fills are gross at the trigger.

## 2026-10-13 — critic round: six study defects fixed
- **As-printed bars.** The grouped, 1-minute and daily calls were `adjusted=true`. A later 1:N reverse split divides
  the old volume by N and multiplies the old prices by N, so a nano-cap that traded 5M shares at $0.30 and then split
  1:20 read as 250k at $6, failed the 1M gate and dropped out. Names that later reverse-split are mostly the failed,
  diluting ones, so the cohort was biased UP. Every fetch now passes `adjusted=false`; the 1-minute call is the
  script's own GET on `_fetch_massive_minute`'s URL and paging (that loader hard-codes `adjusted=true`). Old caches are
  refused by the layout marker.
- **Live scope by default.** Under `warm`, a flag whose leg printed before q was not an event, and the name went into
  the placebo pool (probe: q at 10:04 inside the flag → warm no event, live the 10:15 bull flag). Default is now `live`,
  and under either scope a name enters the placebo only when no setup broke under the live scope.
- **Dated instrument types.** The active-only map read an expired warrant / right / unit as "type unknown" and kept it.
- **Half days** the calendar does not list are skipped (were graded as 16:00 days).
- **Short walks** are counted (requested / got / studied), printed and exit 3 without `--allow-short`.
- **The older-entry-bar guard** (`break_class`: entry bar ≠ the latest closed bar → no event) is now pinned end to end.
- Mutation check: removing that guard, dropping the live-scope placebo check, `adjusted=true` on the 1-minute call, and
  `warm` as the default each turn the suite red (1, 1, 1, 2 failures).
