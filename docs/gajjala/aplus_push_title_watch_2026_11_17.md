  helps it stand out. On Mac Chrome the A+ notification stays until he acts. The worker is served no-cache and updates on
  the next app open.
- **The tap can beat the seat by up to a minute.** The Watch loop seats the name at its NEXT minute step (≤ 60 s after
  the push). A tap inside that minute lands on the Watch without the tile; the board's focus effect re-runs on each poll
  and outlines the tile when it arrives. Outside the loop's window nothing is seated.

  Watch tile links out with its own "view in" link.
