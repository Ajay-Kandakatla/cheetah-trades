| What | Rule | Engine |
|---|---|---|

| The day's bars | the stored read doc when complete, else the 1-minute stand-in | `si_track.day_read` |
| Grade | prior close order, split guard, +`WC.RAN_MIN_PCT` (40) on the 09:30–20:00 high vs that session's own prior close | `si_track.outcome` (the ONE grading engine) |
| Listing-day fact | a 🏁 row with outcome "ok" is COPIED (prior close / high / high % / winner — its split check already passed, no budget); low / close from the day's read | `day_fact` step 1 |
| Off-scanner day | `day_read` FIRST, then `outcome(read_doc=<that read>)` — the stand-in's prior-day tail carries the prior close (handing `outcome` no read gives `no_prev_close` with no load asked: the C2 bug, pinned) | `day_fact` step 2 |
| Low | min `l` of the 09:30–20:00 bars (`s` absent or after hours, start ≥ 09:30) | `si_track.extended_bars` (= runners `_ext`, fill_next's filter) |
| Close | the session's last regular 5-minute close | `si_track._last_regular_close` |
| Base (MINE, §7 #11) | listed before the session, or listed before the regular close → the listing day's last regular close; first listed at or after the regular close (`listed_at` ≥ `_reg_close(day)`, so a half day cuts at 13:00) → the close of the first 5-minute bar starting at or after `listed_at`; first listed with no `listed_at` → NO base (never guessed) | `base_of` |
| Another +40% day | a session whose own `winner` is True | the 🏁 grade |
| New high (MINE) | a later 09:30–20:00 high STRICTLY above the listing day's high | `forward` |
| Run / low / close (MINE) | best high / worst low of the sessions read vs the base; the k-th session's close vs the base | `forward` |
| Did not trade | the provider ANSWERED with no bars for that session (`dt_data.last_fetch_failed()` clear) — a counted row, never a win | `loader` → `no_trade` |
| Split | a terminal split fact stops the numbers (only the sessions before it count) | `forward` |

Load states (`LOADS`): `reads` · `loaded` · `no_bars` (answered empty) · `failed` (`last_fetch_failed()` set, or a
raise) · `deferred` (the budget refused, or the stored-reads-only seam). A fact is retried while its state is in
`si_track.OUTCOME_RETRY`, or the budget refused a unit during it, or a load it used failed — so a `no_prev_close` left
by a refused or failed prior-day load is retried and one after the provider answered is not.

## Why counts wait for a listing day read in full [C3]

A listing day D counts at horizon k only once EVERY name listed on D is read ("ok" or "split") through the k-th
session after D; N = every listed name-day of those days (no-trade, split and no-base rows included). Stored reads
alone are selected on the outcome (§1.5, critic probe 11-25): +1 session is readable for 41 of 43 listing-day
winners (95%) vs 97 of 139 non-winners (70%); +5: 26 of 116 — a later session is stored only when the name
re-qualified on the scanner. Counting "a of N over whatever is read" would count the winners and drop the rest.
Completeness is judged per listing DAY, not per horizon: one stuck name blocks its day, never the whole line. The
not-yet-counted days and their unread name-days are printed. The first-listing-only line (each name's first listed
day) carries the names interval through `scripts.gajjala_study.cluster_ci` at `SIT.MIN_NAMES` (20), else "too few
names for an interval".
