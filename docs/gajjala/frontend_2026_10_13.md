## Behaviour

  has one — a push deep link `?tab=gajjala&focus=SYM` lets the server resolve the sub; the SERVED `sub` is written back
  with history `replace` and no second fetch. An unknown `sub` asks for `bull_flag`.

- **Params at the tab border.** The tab reads `sub` / `tf` / `sort` with its own meanings, and `tabSearch` carried every
  param but `pattern` across a tab switch. Support `tf=15m` → Gajjala's drew his 5-minute setups on 15-minute bars; his
  `tf=5m` → Support opened on the 24-hour frame (`TF_ALIAS`); his `sort=change` reached Back in Demand. `tabSearch` now
  drops `GAJJALA_OWN_PARAMS` (= `GG_SUB_PARAM`, `GG_TF_PARAM`, `GG_SORT_PARAM`, imported) when the switch crosses INTO or
  OUT OF `gajjala`; a click on the tab already open keeps them, and switches between two other tabs are unchanged.
- **`focus` scrolls once.** The param stays in the URL and each served refresh hands a new `tiles` array, so the page
  jumped back to the focused tile every 5 minutes. The scroll is now once per focus value, the first time that tile has
  landed (a ref); the outline stays.

## 🚩 Gajjala's chart on the stock page (2026-11-06)

Ajay 2026-11-06: "If I click on Gajjalas tab stocks show me "Gajjalas chart" in supply and demand of his view by default
mainly.. Like if he looks at 24 hours or 2 week or anything else that is what I would like to see"

- **The link.** The backend serves every 🚩 tile href with the Support-tab frame key of the frame the card is DRAWN on
  (`/sepa/SYM?tab=supply&tf=5m_today`; `&tf=15m` for a card drawn on 15 minutes; `&tf=1min` on the 1-minute page —
  `gajjala/tab.py chart_href`, the map by NAME in `gajjala/constants.CHART_FRAME_KEY_CONST`). The FE composes no href:
  bar-less tiles go through `withSource` (which re-serialises the href's own query, so `tf` survives) and charted tiles
  hand `tile.href` to PatternChart unchanged. Never a bare `tf=5m` — the Support tab's alias sends that to `24h`.
- **The first-paint read.** `pages/SepaCandidate.tsx` reads `?tf=` and `?window=` ONCE, in lazy `useState`
  initialisers, into its plain chart state: `supportTf = parseTf(?tf)` (unknown → daily), `supportTfRetired =
  retiredTf(?tf)` (a retired key → its successor, and `<SupportLevels retired=…>` says so out loud), cleared by the next
  pick in `onWindow` / `onView`. After the first paint the state is plain, as before: a pick is not written back to the
  URL, and `setTab` still copies the params so `?tf=` survives a tab switch.
- **The window rule, and why.** The landed zoom is the link's own `?window=` (`parseWindow`) or the house 1 year
  (`SEPA_SUPPLY_WINDOW`) — never the picker's frame pin (`FRAME_WINDOW['5m_today']` = 6m). SupportLevels remembers the
  window the tab MOUNTED on as the one "The big picture" comes back to (`components/SupportLevels.tsx` `mountWindow`,
  the 2026-09-23 fix); a 6m mount would send daily back at 6 MONTHS, the narrowing that fix removed. Chart Maps lands a
  bare `?tf=` the same way (`pages/ChartMaps.tsx parseWindow(params.get('window'))`). The 5-minute bars drawn are the
  same at 1y or 6m (the server uses the frame's own bar budget on an intraday frame); only the no-cluster daily fallback
  and the board block read 1 year instead of 6 months.
- **Wire.** Landing from a 🚩 card: `GET /chart-maps/support?symbol=BBB&tf=5m_today` (no `window=`; 1y is omitted).
  "The big picture" picked after it: `symbol=BBB` exactly (daily, 1 year). A 15m card: `symbol=BBB&tf=15m`. No `?tf`:
  `symbol=BBB`, as before. `?tf=15m&window=2w` → `window=2w&tf=15m`, and daily keeps 2w.
- **The /sepa → /sepa limit.** `/sepa/:symbol` keys nothing on `:symbol` (App.tsx), so a navigation from one ticker
  page to another re-renders the mounted page and the initialisers do not run again: a second `?tf=` is ignored and the
  page keeps its frame. Today no surface links /sepa → /sepa with `?tf=`; a future one must remount or lift the state.
  Pinned as a NEGATIVE; not "fixed" with an effect on `location.search` (that is URL-mirroring, HIS CALL #5).
- **The averages.** The chart under his frame draws the HOUSE averages (`chart_maps.board.MA_SPECS`: 9 EMA / 20 SMA /
  200 SMA, on every frame), not his 21 EMA — HIS CALL #7.
- **Tests.** `src/pages/SepaCandidate.chartFrame.test.tsx` mounts the real page and reads the support fetch URLs (no
  `?tf`, `5m_today` with no window, 5m_today → "The big picture" = `symbol=X`, `15m`, `15m&window=2w`, `window=2w&tf=daily`,
  garbage tf / window, retired `5m_live` → `24h` + note cleared by a pick, a later pick wins, setTab keeps `tf`;
  NEGATIVES `/sepa/AAA?tf=5m_today` → `/sepa/BBB?tf=15m` keeps `5m_today`, bare `tf=5m` → `24h`).
  `src/components/GajjalaBoard.chart_frame.test.tsx` uses clones of the hand fixture only (tile Links keep `&tf=` to
