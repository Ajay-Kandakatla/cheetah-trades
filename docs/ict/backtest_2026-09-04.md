## Caveats

- Survivorship: the universe is TODAY's big caps (zone_store.big_cap_universe), so names that fell out of it during the span are not tested.
- Fills are the 60m close at the signal bar, not the zone edge; no slippage, no commissions.
- A same-minute touch of both stop and target counts as a STOP (conservative).
- A plan whose target the fill had already passed, or whose stop the fill had already crossed, is 'bad geometry': skipped and counted, never a target hit.
- The placebo line is the placebos of the RESOLVED signals only, each placed so its own window ends before its signal's session.
- The board is read at 60m bar CLOSES only; the cron re-reads every 15 minutes with a partial hour bar, so a state that flickered intra-hour is not a signal here.
- The as-of daily bar is RTH minutes only; the live board overlays the provider's day bar, which can differ by the auction prints.
- The daily history is today's 2y cache cut at each session, so the frame start sits ~18 months before an early signal instead of 24 — only the display-side consolidation count can differ from what the live board computed that day.
- Every threshold is an owner constant unless the video states it; values are listed under `params` and in docs/ict/backtest_method.md.

## Verdict

**Verdict (filled 2026-09-04 04:20 ET).** The board does **not** beat its own placebo. Median ret@horizon +0.03 R vs 0.00 R (0.028 R apart, noise), target-before-stop 35.1% vs 34.5% (0.6 pp), median MFE identical at 0.81 R, median MAE identical at 1.00 R, and the mean expectancy +0.03 R vs −0.04 R — all inside the noise of a 6,004-signal sample. The raw bullish/bearish split (bullish +0.27 R, bearish −0.21 R) is the market, not the setup: the placebo shows the same split (+0.10 / −0.12) and SPY drifted +1.08% median over the same horizons. Grade 100 vs 80, IFVG vs FVG, tap kind, tap agreement and month all sit within a few hundredths of an R of each other and of the placebo. The one bucket that looks different — manipulation at the tapped daily level (−0.12 R, 68.9% stopped) — has 273 resolved rows and is *worse*, not better. The confirmed-but-never-entered table (43.2% target-first) is filled at the confirmation close, which the board never offered, and its median MFE (0.56 R) is lower. No bucket leaned on here has fewer than 30 resolved rows except 2026-09 (0 resolved). Reading: with the current owner constants the ICT tab is a **study board / watchlist filter, not a trade generator** — the same conclusion the supply/demand zone studies reached. Owner levers worth a *proposed* experiment (never silently changed): require the tap bias to agree, drop the daily-level manipulation source, require a wider displacement (DISPLACE_MIN_ATR), and fill at the zone edge instead of the 60m close.
